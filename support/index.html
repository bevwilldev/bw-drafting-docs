Support & Requests

Report a bug, suggest a tool, share one you've built, or ask for help — it goes straight to the Western Sydney Drafting team. Ideas are as welcome as bugs; pick the type below and the form asks only what's relevant. The suite is in early access, so your reports aren't a nuisance — they're the roadmap: what you flag here is what carries it to a fully stable release.

First, try WSYUPDATE in BricsCAD — a fair few issues are already fixed in a newer build. Missing a ribbon or command? Check Troubleshooting first.

Form not loading? You can open it in a new tab instead.

What happens next

  • Your request lands with the Western Sydney Drafting team and gets triaged.
  • Fixes and new tools ship in a suite release — you'll get the update notice inside BricsCAD when one is ready.
  • What changed in each version is listed on the releases page.
